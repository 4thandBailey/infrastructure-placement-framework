# Module 7 — Collaboration Platform and Productivity Strategy

**Pillar:** Run it well · **Framework:** Infrastructure Placement Framework v1.0.0

---

## The question

Is your organisation on the right collaboration platform — or platforms — for how your people actually work, and is that platform properly governed?

---

## Key context

64% of organisations currently run both Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace simultaneously (Flexera 2025). The question is no longer which platform is better. It is which platform earns which use case inside your specific organisation — and whether the platform you are running is configured to deliver its full security, compliance, and cost value.

29% of SaaS licences in the average organisation are unused or underutilised. Data sits unclassified and unprotected in shared drives, Teams channels, and Gmail. Admin privileges are over-granted. Guest access is unmonitored. Copilot and Gemini AI features process organisational data under terms most IT teams have never reviewed.

Governance is 90% of the work.

---

## Platform selection principles

**Microsoft 365** came from the desktop era and layered cloud on top. Its DNA is enterprise-grade control, deep Office file compatibility, and integrated security through Defender, Purview, and Sentinel. Right choice for: heavy Office file dependency, complex compliance requirements, Windows-centric fleets, established enterprise security stacks.

**Google Workspace** was built cloud-native from the browser up. Its DNA is real-time collaboration, simplicity, and speed. Right choice for: cloud-native organisations, real-time co-authoring priority, mixed OS fleets, teams that resist the Outlook paradigm.

**AWS WorkSpaces** targets VDI requirements, remote or contract workforces, or deep AWS investment extending to end-user computing. Not a full collaboration suite replacement — a powerful endpoint delivery mechanism for specific environments.
