## 1. Purpose

This policy defines acceptable use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools by [ORGANISATION NAME] employees, contractors, and any other individuals who use AI tools in connection with their work for the organisation.

AI tools offer significant productivity benefits. They also introduce risks including data exposure, intellectual property disclosure, regulatory non-compliance, and the introduction of inaccurate outputs into business processes. This policy manages those risks while enabling productive AI use.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All employees, contractors, and vendors acting on behalf of [ORGANISATION NAME]
- All AI tools including but not limited to: large language models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot), code generation tools, image generation tools, voice and transcription tools, and AI-embedded features within existing software
- AI tool use on all devices including company-owned and personal devices used for work

## 4. Data classification and AI tools

### 4.1 What you may enter into AI tools

| Data classification | May be entered into approved AI tools? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Yes | No restrictions |
| Internal | Yes, with approved tools only | No customer or employee names |
| Confidential | No, without explicit approval | Requires IT/Legal review and approved enterprise tool with appropriate data terms |
| Restricted / Regulated | Never | Includes PHI, PII, financial data, legal matters, source code, client data |

### 4.2 What you must never enter into AI tools

Regardless of tool or context, do not enter:

- Patient health information (PHI) of any kind
- Client, customer, or patient names combined with any other identifying information
- Financial account numbers, payment card data, or banking information
- Social Security numbers, driver's licence numbers, or government ID numbers
- Proprietary source code, trade secrets, or unpublished product information
- Pending litigation, legal strategy, or attorney-client privileged communications
- Employee personal data, HR records, or disciplinary information
- Contractual terms that are marked confidential
- Any data that would breach a client's non-disclosure agreement

---

## 5. AI on personal devices (BYOD)

If you use a personal device for work:
- You may only use AI tools on that device that are on the approved tool list
- You may not use any AI tool on a personal device to process data classified as Confidential or Restricted
- You acknowledge that AI tools installed as mobile apps may store, process, or transmit data entered into them

---

## 6. Accuracy and verification

AI tools produce outputs that may be inaccurate, outdated, or fabricated (hallucinated). Before using any AI-generated output in a business decision, communication to a client or customer, legal document, or financial record:

- Verify the accuracy of the output independently
- Do not represent AI-generated content as your own expertise without review
- Do not cite AI-generated sources without verifying those sources exist and contain the cited information

---

## 7. Intellectual property

- Do not submit proprietary code, processes, or designs to AI tools that may use inputs for model training
- Content generated by AI tools may not be protected by copyright in the same way as human-authored content — consult Legal before relying on AI-generated content in situations where IP ownership is relevant
- Clearly identify AI-generated content when required by client contracts or applicable guidelines

---

## 8. Unapproved tools

Using AI tools not on the approved list for work purposes is prohibited. If you believe a tool should be evaluated for approval, submit a request to [IT/SECURITY TEAM] at [CONTACT].

IT reserves the right to block access to AI tool domains at the network level. Using circumvention tools (VPNs, proxies) to access blocked AI tools is a violation of this policy.

---

## 9. Reporting

Report any of the following to [IT/SECURITY TEAM] immediately:

- Accidental submission of restricted or confidential data to an AI tool
- Discovery of another employee using AI tools in violation of this policy
- An AI tool that appears to have produced harmful, discriminatory, or legally problematic output that has been used in a business process

---

## 10. Policy violations

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, and may create personal and organisational legal liability.

---

## 11. Review

This policy is reviewed annually or whenever a significant change to AI tools, regulatory requirements, or organisational AI usage makes a review necessary.
