## About this assessment

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0) organises AI governance into four core functions: **Govern**, **Map**, **Measure**, and **Manage**. This assessment scores each function on a scale of 0–25, for a maximum total of 100.

Maturity levels:
| Score | Maturity |
|---|---|
| 0–24 | Initial — no formal AI governance |
| 25–49 | Developing — some controls in place, not systematic |
| 50–74 | Defined — documented governance with gaps |
| 75–89 | Managed — systematic, measured governance |
| 90–100 | Optimising — continuous improvement programme |
