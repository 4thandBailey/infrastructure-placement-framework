**[ORGANISATION NAME] — Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) Security Policy**

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the use of employee-owned personal devices to access [ORGANISATION NAME] data, systems, and networks. It establishes the minimum security requirements for personal devices and defines the organization's rights to manage corporate data on those devices.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and vendors who use a personal device (smartphone, tablet, laptop) to access [ORGANISATION NAME] email, files, applications, or networks.

---

## 3. Eligibility and enrollment

Employees wishing to use a personal device for work must:
1. Enroll the device in [MDM PLATFORM] via [ENROLLMENT PROCESS]
2. Accept this policy and the associated remote wipe scope
3. Meet minimum device specifications (see Section 4)

---

## 4. Minimum device requirements

Personal devices must meet all of the following:

- Running a supported OS version (no end-of-life operating systems)
- Encrypted storage enabled (full-disk encryption on laptops; hardware encryption on mobile)
- Screen lock enabled with PIN, password, or biometric
- Auto-lock set to 5 minutes or less
- Not jailbroken or rooted

---

## 5. Acceptable use

Employees may use personal devices for:
- [ORGANISATION NAME] email and calendar
- Approved collaboration tools (Teams, Slack, etc.)
- Approved cloud storage access
- Approved productivity applications

---

## 6. AI tools on personal devices

Employees using personal devices for work must comply with the [ORGANISATION NAME] AI Acceptable Use Policy. Specifically:

- Only AI tools on the [ORGANISATION NAME] approved AI tool list may be used on personal devices for work purposes
- No restricted or confidential [ORGANISATION NAME] data may be entered into any AI tool on a personal device
- AI apps installed for personal use that process work data are subject to the AI Acceptable Use Policy

---

## 7. Organization rights

By enrolling a personal device, the employee acknowledges that [ORGANISATION NAME] has the right to:

- Remotely wipe **corporate data only** (selective wipe) from the enrolled device
- Monitor compliance status of the device (encryption, OS version, PIN enforcement)
- Remotely lock the corporate work profile in the event of a security incident

**[ORGANISATION NAME] does NOT have the right to:**
- Access personal files, photos, messages, or non-corporate applications
- Perform a full device wipe without the employee's explicit consent (except in cases of confirmed data breach — see Section 8)

---

## 8. Lost, stolen, or compromised devices

If a personal device is lost, stolen, or believed to be compromised:
1. Report immediately to [IT SECURITY CONTACT] at [CONTACT]
2. IT will perform a selective wipe of corporate data
3. In the event of confirmed data breach, a full device wipe may be required — employee will be notified before execution except in circumstances where immediate wipe is required by legal obligation

---

## 9. Departure from the organization

Upon termination of employment or contract:
- Corporate data and applications will be remotely removed from the personal device
- MDM enrollment will be removed
- Personal data is not affected
