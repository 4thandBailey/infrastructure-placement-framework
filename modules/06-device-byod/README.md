# Module 6 — End-User Device and BYOD Strategy

**Pillar:** Run it well · **Framework:** Infrastructure Placement Framework v1.0.0

---

## The question

Are your devices governed under the right ownership model for each role — and does your device policy explicitly address the fastest-growing shadow AI vector in 2026?

---

## The four ownership models

| Model | Who owns it | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| BYOD — Bring Your Own Device | Employee | Knowledge workers with low data sensitivity, cost-sensitive environments |
| CYOD — Choose Your Own Device | Organization (employee selects from approved list) | Balance of preference and control |
| COPE — Corporate-Owned, Personally Enabled | Organization | Roles with moderate-to-high data sensitivity who need personal flexibility |
| COBO — Corporate-Owned, Business Only | Organization | High-security roles, regulated environments, field operations |

---

## The AI-on-personal-device problem

Employees are using ChatGPT, Gemini, and AI-embedded mobile apps to process work data on personal devices. This is the newest and fastest-growing shadow AI vector, and it connects directly back to Module 5. The BYOD policy must explicitly address which AI tools are permitted on devices that access corporate data — and the MDM controls that enforce that policy.
